Your game stays on your device

The public Android game works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, in-app purchases or cloud saves. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, photos or gameplay records.

Progress, virtual Treat balances, preferences, rewards and interrupted-round recovery are stored locally on your device. These records are used only to run and resume your game.
