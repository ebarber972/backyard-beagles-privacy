Privacy Policy for Backyard Beagles

Effective Date: November 13, 2026
Package ID: com.ebarber972.beagles

1. Overview

Backyard Beagles is an offline, free-to-play slot-style game. The application operates 100% locally on your Android device and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or device data.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: We do not collect names, emails, location, device IDs, or diagnostics.
  • No Internet Access: The app does not request internet permissions and communicates with no external servers.
  • Local Storage Only: Your virtual "Treats" balance, high scores, and preferences are saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Simulated Gambling & Virtual "Treats"

All in-game currency ("Treats") is virtual play money only. There are no in-app purchases, no real-money wagers, and no cash or real-world prizes.

4. Third-Party Services

No third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs are included in the app.

5. Contact

Questions? Contact the developer at https://github.com/ebarber972/backyard-beagles.
